ABOUT RCAMP

Control belongs
closer to the device.

RCAMP makes local hardware control feel as considered as the hardware itself: clear, composable, and open to the community.

Local-first

Control over your network, Bluetooth, or USB—without an online account.

Open standards

Established transports first; proprietary protocols only when necessary.

One core

Rust is the source of truth for GUI, Android, and RCAMP/CLI.

Build with the project.

Open an issue, propose an adapter, or contribute to the core.
